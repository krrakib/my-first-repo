FAQ

Questions

Can I use the sprites in a commercial game?

Yes. Free sprites from the generator need a small credit ("Sprites by Spriteloom"). Sprites from paid packs need no credit at all. You can't resell the sprites themselves as an asset pack.

Which engines does this work with?

Anything that loads PNG: Godot, Unity, GameMaker, Unreal (Paper2D), Phaser, PixiJS, Love2D, Pygame, RPG Maker and more. Packs also include JSON atlases in the TexturePacker format.

How do I keep the pixels sharp?

Turn off texture filtering. In Godot set Default Texture Filter to Nearest. In Unity set Filter Mode to Point and Compression to None.

Why buy a pack if the generator is free?

Packs save you hours: hundreds of hand-picked sprites with a consistent style, sorted into folders, exported at two sizes, with spritesheets and atlases ready to import, and no credit needed.

Can I request a sprite type?

Yes. All-packs owners vote on the next pack, and new generators ship every month.
